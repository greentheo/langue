> **What's new in Langue this week**
>
> Langue has changed a lot this week. The short version: **press "Next
> step" and it takes it from there.**
>
> 🧭 **A guided path.** One button plans your next step, each planned for
> about three minutes: new words, reviews that are due, and the ones you
> keep missing. New words climb from picking the meaning, to typing them, to
> using them in sentences and little role-plays.
>
> 📚 **A1 and A2 are complete** in Italian, Spanish, French and Brazilian
> Portuguese: about a thousand words, plus phrases, grammar and 16
> role-plays per level.
>
> 📈 **A level page.** Bars for words, phrases, grammar and conversations,
> which only ever go up. Each unit ends with a level-up challenge, and if you
> already know a unit, you can test out of it. Finish a level and you get a
> certificate and a badge on the leaderboard.
>
> 🏆 **A leaderboard for every group you're in.** Each invite code is its own
> group, and you can join more groups with a code from the leaderboard page.
>
> 📱 **Put it on your home screen.** Langue now installs like an app. On iPhone:
> Share, then Add to Home Screen. (On iPhone you'll sign in once more inside
> the app.)
>
> 🔔 **Reminders, if you want them.** Turn them on and you'll get a nudge
> after two quiet days, then five, then twelve, and then they stop. And your
> streak is never taken away: a break doesn't reset it.
>
> 🔊 **Sounds.** A chime for right and wrong. New words and the other
> person's lines in role-plays are read aloud in your device's voice, with a
> speaker button to hear them again. There's one switch for all of it.
>
> **One thing to know:** grading is stricter now, so now and then a right
> answer will be marked wrong. When it happens, send the question and what
> you typed to {owner}. That's how it gets fixed, and a "report this" button
> is planned.
>
> Happy learning!

`{owner}` is the owner's own first name. The draft doesn't use it because
this repo is public.
